Take your photos out of Google Photos, and know that it worked.

Pomegranate is an Android app that shows where each of your photos and videos lives, copies the ones that exist only in Google Photos to storage you choose, and checks every copy.

  • On this phone only
  • In Google Photos only
  • In both places

What it does

  • Shows where everything lives. Every photo and video is marked as on this phone, in Google Photos, or both.
  • Copies what is missing. You pick items in Google Photos; Pomegranate downloads them to a folder you choose on internal storage, an SD card or a USB drive.
  • Proves every copy. Each downloaded file is verified and listed in a manifest you can export.
  • Is honest about quality. Google's picker removes location from photos and re-encodes videos. Every file is labelled with what you actually got.
  • Leaves no trace. One tap disconnects from Google, revokes the app's access and wipes everything the app knew about your Google Photos library.

Pomegranate never deletes anything, on your phone or in Google Photos.

How it uses your Google account

Pomegranate asks for one Google permission: read-only access to the items you select yourself in the Google Photos picker. It uses that access for a transfer you start: to show the items you picked, to match them with the files already on your phone, and to download them to the folder you chose.

It cannot see the rest of your library, and it cannot change or delete anything in Google Photos. You can remove its access at any time from inside the app, or from your Google Account.

Private by design

The developer never receives any of your data. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app talks only to Google, and only when you ask it to.

The details are in the privacy policy.

Why the name

In the myth, Persephone eats a few pomegranate seeds in the underworld and finds she can no longer leave for good. Free storage can work the same way: hospitality that quietly takes away your choice to go. Pomegranate is for getting your photos back out.

Availability

Pomegranate is for Android 13 and later. It is free, has no ads, and is being prepared for release on Google Play.
